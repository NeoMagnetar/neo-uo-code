# Region geometry and boundaries

Physical definition of arena space. These rules establish bounds, z-limits, containment layers, and boundary behavior for rings, lobbies, shells, and observer spaces.

## Scope

This chapter governs where the arena begins and ends, how nested regions are structured, and what happens when players or creatures touch invalid edges.
