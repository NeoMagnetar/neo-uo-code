# Entry, exit, and routing

Ingress and egress rules for fighters, spectators, staff, and eliminated players. This chapter turns abstract arena spaces into controlled movement routes.

## Scope

This chapter governs who may enter, from where they may enter, where they are routed after elimination, and how exit gates and teleporters enforce legality.
