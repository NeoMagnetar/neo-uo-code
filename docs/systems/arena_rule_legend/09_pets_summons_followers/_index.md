# Pets, summons, and followers

Follower-side governance for bonded pets, summons, escorts, companions, and uncontrolled creatures that could distort arena fairness.

## Scope

This chapter governs whether pets and summons may enter, remain, heal, attack, count toward eligibility, or be forcibly removed from the arena space.
