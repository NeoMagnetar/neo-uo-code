# Item, equipment, and consumable

Loadout governance for potions, gear locks, naked duels, consumable posture, bandage exceptions, and legal-vs-illegal equipment handling.

## Scope

This chapter governs what may be equipped or used before and during a match, what gets stripped or bounced to backpack, and whether consumables are free or normal-cost.
