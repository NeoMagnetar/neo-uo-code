# Death, corpse, resurrection, and loot

Post-death meaning inside the arena: elimination style, corpse treatment, ghost routing, post-match resurrection, loot posture, and rematch restoration.

## Scope

This chapter governs what happens when a fighter dies, where ghosts or corpses go, whether resurrection is legal in-match, and what gets restored after rounds or rematches.
