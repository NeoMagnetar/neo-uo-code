# Spectator, outsider, and interference

Protection against non-fighter contamination of the arena. This chapter separates stands, observer decks, outsider routing, and interference law.

## Scope

This chapter governs what spectators and outsiders may see, say, target, heal, toss, loot, summon, or cross while fighters are active.
